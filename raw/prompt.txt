Rules (Each with a little pixelated icon):
Every night is a double feature. First kids, then adults. Before each movie is a short cartoon. Between movies is a short intermission!
Just like at the drive-in, we will transmit the audio for the movie over stereo FM radio. Bring your own a boom box or walkman or whatever, and listen along!
If you or your kids are making trouble or hurting people/animals, you’ll be asked to leave. End of story.
Bring a flashlight for when it gets dark, but don’t shine it during the movie.
We have a swingset and a chicken coop. Kids can play when they need to!
No pets allowed. We have cats and chickens that will get upset.
Movies begin at dusk. Come earlier to get a good spot!
Bring your own blanket, picnic and drinks. Some alcohol is OK! But don’t push it.
If you have to use the bathroom, you can go into the woods behind the house. Toilet paper and shovels will be provided.
BBQing allowed!
